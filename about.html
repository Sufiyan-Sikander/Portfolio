About Me

Hey 👋🏼 I'm Sufiyan Sikander, an AI Engineer based in Karachi 🇵🇰, passionate about building intelligent systems and scalable AI-powered applications that solve real-world problems.

I specialize in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, and Computer Vision, with hands-on experience in developing full-stack AI solutions using technologies like Python, FastAPI, React.js, TensorFlow, LangChain, Pinecone, Hugging Face, and OpenCV.

I've worked on multiple impactful projects, including TrafficGPT — an AI-powered traffic assistant featuring chat support, challan calculation, license guidance, and real-time traffic/weather alerts. I also developed SmartGuard, an AI humanoid security robot with real-time face recognition and autonomous movement, along with ANN-based analytics systems for customer prediction and business insights.

Alongside my projects, I've gained industry experience as an AI Intern at E-services and Technologies, where I worked on Docker environments, authentication systems, chat modules, and AI research. I've also worked as an AI STEM Instructor at Align STEM Club, conducting AI and robotics workshops while mentoring students in Custom GPTs, robotics, and computer vision technologies.

Currently, I'm focused on building Agentic AI systems and exploring MCP (Model Context Protocol) Servers to create more scalable and autonomous AI ecosystems.
